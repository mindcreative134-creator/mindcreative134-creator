## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hello and welcome! I am **Keshav** (*MindCreative*), an **Android & Media Systems Developer** passionate about architecting high-performance video players, native media engines, and full-featured Android applications. 

- 🎬 **Flagship Creation**: Creator & Lead Maintainer of **[NextPlayer](https://github.com/mindcreative134-creator/nextplayer)** — a revolutionary hybrid Android player fusing **libmpv** and **AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer)** with Anime4K shaders and integrated Torrentio streaming.
- 📱 **Mobile Ecosystems**: Architect of production-grade native Android tools, cross-platform Android TV solutions (Flutter/Dart), and automated Web-to-Native application engines.
- ⚡ **Engineering Focus**: Low-level native JNI/C++ performance, custom GLSL shader post-processing, audio decoding pipelines (FFmpeg, Dolby, 7.1 surround), and clean reactive architecture.
